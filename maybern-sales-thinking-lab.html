The Core Principle
Advancing questions carry Corporate Visions buyer psychology — they shift belief and disrupt status quo comfort. Confirming questions carry MEDDPICC checks — they validate the deal is actually moving. Both matter. Most AEs only use one.
Module 1

Question Upgrade Engine

Each exercise shows a raw question from a Maybern deal. First classify it — does it advance the deal or confirm something you already know? Then rewrite it. Then see how an elite AE would ask it.

Module 2

Urgency Reframe Lab

Value without risk does not create urgency. Each exercise starts with efficiency-based messaging. Rewrite it at the level that actually moves deals.

Level 1 — Operational
A time-bound consequence tied to a real event: exit, audit, fund launch, carry distribution. Makes waiting dangerous.
Level 2 — GP Accountability
Shifts the problem from "this is inefficient" to "this is my accountability." Breaks status quo comfort. Turns nice-to-have into must-have.
Module 3

Status Quo Disruption Builder

A buyer who feels safe doesn't buy. Each scenario shows a common buyer belief or defense. Choose your response. The system scores whether you disrupted the belief or reinforced it.

Module 4

Complexity Risk Simulator

Most funds don't struggle with the math. They struggle with the logic. Each exercise surfaces a fund complexity driver. Your job is to communicate the risk — not the feature.

Core Distinction
Features describe capability. Risk describes what happens when that capability isn't there during a realization, an audit, or an LP question. Always sell risk first.
Module 5

Deal Advancement Tracker

Use the per-meeting MEDDPICC checklist to stress-test your deals. Every unchecked box is a gap. If you can't check it, the deal hasn't advanced — it's only confirmed.
